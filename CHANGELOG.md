# Changelog

All notable changes to this repository, by date. Entries summarise what changed for a reader; the Git history has the full detail.

## 2026-10-29

### Added

- **Cheat sheets:** fixed income, credit risk, technical indicators, linear algebra, probability, C++ quick reference, and market microstructure. A cheat-sheet index.
- **Projects:** full specs for projects 04–13, plus a spec template (`projects/TEMPLATE.md`).
- **Project 14:** finite-difference Black-Scholes solver. **Project 15:** technical-indicator study.
- **Roadmaps:** Quant Trader and Risk and Model Validation roadmaps, a roadmap index, and time budgets and cheat-sheet links in each roadmap.
- **Tools and technologies:** eight files covering Python, C++, profiling, Linux, version control, databases and quant libraries.
- **Interview banks:** brainteasers, statistics and ML, options and derivatives, market-making and betting games, and C++ and data structures.
- **Resources:** books, papers and lectures, with verdicts and the phases that use them.
- **Reference:** a glossary, a FAQ, and a guide to Indian market structure (`careers/india-markets.md`).

### Changed

- **Data sources:** rewritten with checked dates, a source-selection table, corporate-action guidance, and a provenance template.
- **Careers:** each firm has its own row with a link, a verdict and a checked date. Vague rows removed.
- **Project ladder:** status key corrected; rows 04–13 link to their specs.
- **Root README:** links to the new folders, and the Quant Trader and Risk roadmaps.

### Fixed

- Corrected formulas and statements in the cheat sheets, including the ATM option approximation and the Black-76 delta conversion, after checking them numerically.
- Removed or corrected several claims that could not be verified, including a Kaggle competition attribution and a CCI constant.
