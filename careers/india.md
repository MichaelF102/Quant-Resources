## Entry routes

1. **On-campus placements** — the main route at IITs and a few other campuses for HFTs. If your campus isn't on their list, use the routes below.
2. **Off-campus applications** — apply directly via careers pages; referrals from alumni substantially improve response rates.
3. **Competitions** — a strong result can stand in for a target-school CV. See the table below.
4. **Internships** — most HFT and fund hiring at entry level converts from internships; applications often open 9–12 months ahead.
5. **Adjacent roles** — risk analytics, model validation and data engineering at banks are realistic stepping stones into front-office quant work.

## Making your profile stand out

- Two projects from the [ladder](../projects/) written up properly: hypothesis, method, results, and what didn't work.
- A clean GitHub with tests and READMEs — reviewers open your code.
- Competition rankings or certifications only if they're strong; a mediocre rank adds little.
- For QD roles: demonstrable C++ (an order book or matching engine) beats a list of languages.
