# Books

*Last reviewed: Oct 2026*

A single list of the books recommended across the roadmaps and cheat sheets, with a verdict, the phases that use them, and a cost tag. Read the verdict before buying. A book in the "Primary" column of a roadmap is worth the time; a book listed as a reference is for looking things up.

**Cost tags** follow the roadmaps. They describe typical availability, not current prices. Check the publisher's page before buying. 🆓 means a free version exists (author site or open-access edition). 💸 means paid.

## Probability and statistics

| Book | Verdict | Level | Cost | Used in |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Blitzstein and Hwang, *Introduction to Probability* | Best intuition-first probability text. Pair it with the Stat 110 lectures. | 🟢→🟡 | 💸 | QR Phase 1 |
| Wasserman, *All of Statistics* | Terse and wide. A good second book on estimation and testing. | 🟡 | 💸 | QR Phase 1 (backup) |
| Zhou, *A Practical Guide to Quantitative Finance Interviews* | Interview-style problems with worked solutions. Work every problem. | 🟡 | 💸 | QR Phase 1, QT Phase 2 |
| Mosteller, *Fifty Challenging Problems in Probability* | Short classics. Many interview questions come from here. | 🟡 | 💸 | QT Phase 2, QD interviews |
| Crack, *Heard on the Street* | Older and broader. Useful as a second pass on problems. | 🟡 | 💸 | Interview prep |
| Joshi, *Quant Job Interview Questions and Answers* | Stronger on derivatives and C++. Best for desk-quant roles. | 🟡 | 💸 | Interview prep |
| Mathematical puzzle collections (Gardner, Winkler) | Good for brainteaser practice. Not for finance theory. | 🟢 | 💸 | Brainteasers |

## Reading order suggestions

- **Researcher:** Blitzstein and Hwang, then Strang, then Tsay, then Hull with Shreve, then ISLR and López de Prado.
- **Developer:** learncpp (free course), Meyers, Williams, OSTEP, then Harris and Kleppmann if you target research platforms.
- **Trader:** Zhou and Mosteller alongside Natenberg, then Sinclair, Taleb and Dixit and Nalebuff.
- **Risk:** ISLR, Hull, McNeil, Frey and Embrechts, then Lando.

See the [papers list](papers.md) for the academic articles these books draw on.
